# Phases

The work in order, with the IDs each phase covers in the other logs. A phase ends when the user says so; its ranges are filled in then. Roadmap milestones from `ROADMAP.md` become phases of their own, under their `M` IDs.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Kickoff | `/brief`, then `/kickoff`: `brief.md`, `SPEC.md`, `PLAN.md`, `ROADMAP.md` | from P-031 | from D-11 | from I-11 | from C-25 |
